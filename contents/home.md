
#### Biography
I am currently a second-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), Wuhan, China, expecting to graduate in 2028. My research focuses on continual learning for large language models — enabling models to acquire new knowledge and skills over time without catastrophic forgetting. More broadly, I am interested in how models can cope with the dynamics of real-world data. My earlier work explored this question on temporal graphs, event sequences, and temporal point processes, and I continue to draw on that background in my current work on continually adapting LLMs to evolving distributions and tasks.
